Top Destinations

Uganda 🦍

Gorilla Trekking

Bwindi forest experience with mountain gorillas.

Uganda 🏜️ Kidepo Valley

Wild, remote safari experience.

Kidepo Valley is Uganda’s most remote national park, famous for its untouched wilderness, lions, cheetahs, ostriches, and dramatic savannah landscapes near the borders of South Sudan and Kenya.

Uganda 🌿 Semuliki

Hot springs & rainforest adventure.

Semuliki is a lowland tropical forest with rich biodiversity, famous for the Sempaya hot springs, birds, monkeys, and unique Congo-like rainforest ecosystem.

Uganda 🐒 Kibale Forest

Chimpanzee capital of the world.

Kibale Forest is home to over 1,500 chimpanzees and 13 primate species, offering the best chimp tracking experience in East Africa.

Uganda 🦍 Mgahinga

Golden monkeys & volcanoes.

Located in the Virunga mountains, Mgahinga offers gorilla trekking, golden monkey tracking, and hiking the volcanic peaks of Muhabura, Gahinga, and Sabinyo.

Uganda 🦁 Queen Elizabeth

Tree climbing lions & boat cruises.

Famous for tree-climbing lions in Ishasha, boat safaris on Kazinga Channel, and elephants, hippos, and buffaloes in large numbers.

Uganda 💦 Murchison Falls

Nile River safari.

The Nile River squeezes through a narrow gorge creating powerful waterfalls, surrounded by elephants, lions, and giraffes.

Kenya 🦩 Lake Nakuru

Flamingo paradise.

Famous for thousands of flamingos, rhinos, and beautiful lake scenery surrounded by acacia forests.

Kenya 🐘 Maasai Mara

Great Migration safari.

One of Africa’s most famous safari destinations, known for the Great Wildebeest Migration and the Big Five wildlife experience.

Kenya 🌄 Amboseli

Views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Famous for large elephant herds with the backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa’s highest peak.

Tanzania 🦓 Serengeti

Endless wildlife plains.

Known for vast savannahs, predator action, and the Great Migration of millions of wildebeests and zebras.

Tanzania 🐘 Tarangire

Elephant herds & baobab trees.

Known for massive elephant herds, ancient baobab trees, and rich wildlife especially during dry seasons.

Tanzania 🌋 Ngorongoro

World’s largest volcanic caldera.

A natural wonder filled with lions, rhinos, elephants, and flamingos inside a collapsed volcano crater.

Rwanda 🦍 Volcanoes

Gorilla trekking experience.

Home to endangered mountain gorillas and golden monkeys, with stunning volcanic mountain scenery.
