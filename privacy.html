Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 4, 2026

Welcome to Pearl Dreams Safaris. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled safely, securely, and responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you visit our website or use our travel and safari services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with our website or book our services:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Country of residence
  • Passport details (only when required for travel arrangements)
  • Emergency contact information
  • Payment and billing information
  • Travel preferences and itinerary details
  • Information submitted through contact or booking forms

We may also collect technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited on our website
  • Date and time of website visits

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process safari and tour bookings
  • Arrange accommodation and transportation
  • Secure permits for activities such as gorilla trekking
  • Respond to inquiries and customer support requests
  • Send booking confirmations and travel updates
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Prevent fraud and enhance website security
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Payment Security

Pearl Dreams Safaris works with trusted payment providers to process secure transactions. We do not store your complete debit or credit card details on our servers. All online payments are processed using secure encryption technologies.

4. Sharing Your Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell or rent your personal information.

Your information may only be shared with trusted partners when necessary:

  • Hotels and safari lodges
  • National park authorities
  • Licensed tour guides
  • Transportation providers
  • Payment processors
  • Government agencies where required by law

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve performance, remember preferences, analyze traffic, and enhance user experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

6. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data. However, no online system is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

  • Access your personal information
  • Request corrections
  • Request deletion where legally permitted
  • Withdraw consent
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Ask questions about data usage

8. Third-Party Links

We are not responsible for privacy practices of external websites linked from our platform.

9. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18 without parental consent.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. Updates will appear on this page with a new effective date.
