🌍 10-Day Safari Itinerary

Experience the ultimate East African adventure with Pearl Dreams Safaris — carefully designed for wildlife, culture, and luxury.

Day 1 — Arrival in Uganda 🇺🇬

Airport pickup and hotel check-in.

Meet your guide, receive safari briefing, and relax at your lodge.

Day 2 — Kampala City Tour 🏙️

Explore Uganda’s capital city.

Visit cultural sites, markets, and historical landmarks.

Day 3 — Lake Victoria 🌊

Boat cruise experience.

Enjoy sunset views and visit fishing villages.

Day 4 — Murchison Falls 🦁

Game drive adventure.

See elephants, lions, giraffes, and powerful waterfalls.

Day 5 — Boat Safari 🚤

Nile River cruise.

Spot hippos, crocodiles, and rare birds.

Day 6 — Transfer to Bwindi Forest 🌿

Journey to gorilla land.

Drive through scenic hills and villages.

Day 7 — Gorilla Trekking 🦍

Meet mountain gorillas.

Experience one of the rarest wildlife encounters on earth.

Day 8 — Cultural Experience 🏕️

Visit local communities.

Enjoy traditional dances and storytelling.

Day 9 — Queen Elizabeth Park 🐘

Big Five safari drive.

Lions, elephants, buffalo, and scenic landscapes.

Day 10 — Departure ✈️

Farewell Uganda.

Transfer to airport with unforgettable memories.
