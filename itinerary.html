10 Days Uganda Wildlife & Primates Safari

Day 1 — Arrival at Entebbe ✈️

Welcome to the Pearl of Africa.

Meet your professional safari guide, transfer to your hotel, enjoy a welcome dinner, and relax after your flight.

Day 2 — Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary 🦏

Rhino tracking experience.

Track endangered white rhinos on foot before continuing to Murchison Falls National Park.

Day 3 — Murchison Falls Safari 🦁

Morning game drive.

Search for lions, elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, antelopes, and enjoy spectacular views of the savannah.

Day 4 — Top of the Falls & Nile Cruise 🚤

Discover the mighty Nile.

Take a boat safari to the base of Murchison Falls and hike to the top for breathtaking views.

Day 5 — Kibale Forest 🌳

Journey to the primate capital.

Travel through scenic countryside to Kibale Forest National Park, home to 13 primate species.

Day 6 — Chimpanzee Trekking 🐒

Meet our closest relatives.

Track wild chimpanzees in Kibale Forest and enjoy an afternoon nature walk through Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary.

Day 7 — Queen Elizabeth National Park 🐘

Game drive and wildlife viewing.

Spot elephants, buffaloes, Uganda kobs, hyenas, and many bird species across the park.

Day 8 — Kazinga Channel Cruise 🛶

Boat safari adventure.

Cruise along the Kazinga Channel to see hippos, crocodiles, elephants, and hundreds of water birds.

Day 9 — Lake Mburo National Park 🦓

Walking safari and game drive.

Enjoy a guided walking safari and encounter zebras, impalas, elands, giraffes, and beautiful acacia woodlands.

Day 10 — Return to Entebbe 🚗

End of your unforgettable safari.

Stop at the Equator for photos and souvenir shopping before your airport transfer for departure.

7 Days Uganda Wildlife & Gorilla Safari

Day 1 — Arrival in Entebbe ✈️

Welcome to the Pearl of Africa.

Meet your professional safari guide at Entebbe International Airport, transfer to your hotel, receive a safari briefing, and relax before your adventure begins.

Day 2 — Transfer to Murchison Falls National Park 🦁

Journey through Uganda's countryside.

Drive to Murchison Falls National Park with a stop at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary for on-foot rhino tracking before checking into your safari lodge.

Day 3 — Game Drive & Nile Boat Safari 🚤

Explore Uganda's largest national park.

Enjoy an early morning game drive to spot lions, elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, and antelopes, followed by an afternoon boat cruise to the base of Murchison Falls.

Day 4 — Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park 🐘

Scenic drive through western Uganda.

Travel to Queen Elizabeth National Park, enjoying breathtaking views of tea plantations, crater lakes, and the Rwenzori Mountains along the way.

Day 5 — Kazinga Channel Cruise & Evening Game Drive 🛶

Wildlife on land and water.

Cruise along the famous Kazinga Channel to see hippos, crocodiles, elephants, and countless birds before heading out for an evening game drive.

Day 6 — Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi 🦍

An unforgettable primate encounter.

Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park for a once-in-a-lifetime gorilla trekking experience and spend an hour observing a mountain gorilla family.

Day 7 — Return to Entebbe & Departure ✈️

End of your safari adventure.

Drive back to Entebbe with a stop at the Uganda Equator for photos, souvenir shopping, and lunch before your airport transfer for departure.

3 Days Queen Elizabeth Wildlife Safari

Day 1 — Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park 🐘

Begin your safari adventure.

Depart from Entebbe or Kampala in the morning and enjoy a scenic drive through western Uganda. Arrive at your lodge in Queen Elizabeth National Park, relax, and head out for an evening game drive to spot elephants, buffaloes, antelopes, and more.

Day 2 — Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise 🚤

Experience the park's incredible wildlife.

Start with an early morning game drive in search of lions, leopards, hyenas, elephants, and Uganda kobs. In the afternoon, enjoy a boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel, famous for its hippos, crocodiles, elephants, and diverse birdlife.

Day 3 — Return to Entebbe ✈️

End of your safari.

After breakfast, check out of your lodge and drive back to Entebbe or Kampala. Stop at the Equator for photos, lunch, and souvenir shopping before your departure.

3 Days Uganda & Rwanda Gorilla Safari

Day 1 — Kigali City Tour & Transfer to Bwindi 🇷🇼➡️🇺🇬

From Rwanda to Uganda.

Arrive at Kigali International Airport where your guide will welcome you. Enjoy a short Kigali city tour, including local markets and historical landmarks, before crossing the Cyanika or Katuna border into Uganda. Continue to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, check in to your lodge, and relax for the evening.

Day 2 — Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi 🦍

Once-in-a-lifetime wildlife encounter.

After an early morning briefing, set off into Bwindi Forest to track the endangered mountain gorillas. Spend one magical hour observing a gorilla family in their natural habitat before returning to your lodge to relax and celebrate your achievement.

Day 3 — Return to Kigali & Departure ✈️

Scenic drive back to Rwanda.

After breakfast, drive back through the beautiful hills of southwestern Uganda into Rwanda. Depending on your flight time, enjoy souvenir shopping or lunch in Kigali before your transfer to Kigali International Airport for your departure.

4 Days Bwindi Gorilla Trekking Safari

Day 1 — Arrival & Transfer to Bwindi 🌿

Journey to the home of mountain gorillas.

Meet your guide in Entebbe or Kampala and drive through the beautiful countryside to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, with stops at the Equator and local craft markets.

Day 2 — Gorilla Trekking Adventure 🦍

Meet the gentle giants.

After an early briefing, trek through the rainforest in search of a mountain gorilla family. Spend one unforgettable hour observing these incredible primates before returning to your lodge.

Day 3 — Batwa Cultural Experience 🪘

Explore local traditions.

Visit the Batwa community, learn about their forest heritage, enjoy traditional music and dance, and take a guided nature walk around the park.

Day 4 — Return to Entebbe ✈️

Farewell to Uganda.

Enjoy breakfast before driving back to Entebbe or Kampala. Stop at the Uganda Equator for photos and souvenir shopping before your airport transfer.

10 Days Uganda Adventure & Wildlife Safari

Day 1 — Arrival in Entebbe ✈️

Airport meet and greet.

Your safari guide will welcome you at Entebbe International Airport and transfer you to your hotel for dinner and an overnight stay.

Day 2 — Jinja & Source of the Nile 🌊

Discover the adventure capital.

Visit the Source of the Nile, enjoy a boat ride, explore local craft markets, and experience the vibrant atmosphere of Jinja.

Day 3 — Sipi Falls ⛰️

Scenic waterfalls and coffee tour.

Hike to the beautiful Sipi Falls, enjoy panoramic views of Mount Elgon, and visit a local coffee farm.

Day 4 — Pian Upe Wildlife Reserve 🦒

Hidden wildlife gem.

Search for giraffes, ostriches, antelopes, cheetahs, and enjoy the untouched wilderness of northeastern Uganda.

Day 5 — Kidepo Valley National Park 🦁

Explore Uganda's wild frontier.

Arrive in Kidepo Valley National Park and enjoy an evening game drive in one of Africa's most spectacular parks.

Day 6 — Full-Day Kidepo Safari 🐘

Exceptional wildlife viewing.

Spend the day tracking lions, elephants, buffaloes, zebras, leopards, and a variety of bird species across the Narus Valley.

Day 7 — Karamojong Cultural Visit 🪘

Experience local traditions.

Visit a traditional Karamojong village, learn about their unique culture, dances, livestock traditions, and daily life.

Day 8 — Return to Gulu 🌅

Scenic drive through northern Uganda.

Travel south through beautiful landscapes and relax at your lodge in Gulu with an evening at leisure.

Day 9 — Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary 🦏

On-foot rhino tracking.

Enjoy a guided rhino tracking experience before continuing to Entebbe for your final overnight stay.

Day 10 — Departure ✈️

End of your Ugandan adventure.

Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy some leisure time before your transfer to Entebbe International Airport.
